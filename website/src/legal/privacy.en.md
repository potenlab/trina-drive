Trina Inc. (주식회사 트리나, "**we**") provides Trina Drive services and the website https://drive.trina.app. This policy explains what personal information we collect about our customers, website visitors and people who contact us, why, and what rights you have. It is written to meet Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and, for visitors in the EU/UK, the GDPR.

**This policy does not cover the files you store in Trina Drive.** For those files you are the controller and we act only as your processor, under §11 of our Terms of Service:
- **Self-hosted or Enterprise on-site:** Trina Drive runs on your own hardware. When we operate it for you, we handle personal data in your files only as your processor.
- **Trina Cloud (hosted by us):** your files, permissions database and audit log are stored and processed by Trina Inc. in your team's own instance, on infrastructure provided by Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the United States, which acts as our sub-processor (see §5). *Draft: Trina Cloud is in early access; this section will be finalized before paid sign-ups open.*

## 2. Cookies

The website uses only cookies that are strictly necessary (for example, to keep the checkout working). The checkout window opened by Paddle sets its own cookies under Paddle's policy. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the checkout may then not work.

## 3. Sharing with third parties

We share personal information only: (a) with the processors listed in §5 so they can perform services for us; (b) with **Paddle.com Market Limited**, which acts as our reseller and Merchant of Record for online orders — when you buy through our online checkout, Paddle collects your payment and billing details and processes them as an independent controller under its own privacy policy (https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy), and shares with us the order details we need (name, email, company, country, tax ID, items purchased); (c) when required by law or a valid order from a public authority; (d) to protect the rights, property or safety of our customers or others.

## 4. AI providers you connect

If you connect an AI client (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, etc.) to your Trina Drive server, the content the agent reads goes from your server to that AI provider under your own agreement with them. We are not a party to that transfer.

## 6. How we delete information

When the retention period ends or the purpose is achieved, we delete electronic records so they cannot be recovered and shred paper records. Records we must keep by law (such as tax records) are stored separately and kept only for the required period.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or stop processing your personal information, to withdraw consent, and (in the EU/UK) to port your data or object to processing. Email contact@trina.app; we will respond within 10 days (PIPA) or one month (GDPR). You may also have a representative make the request. We may need to verify your identity. If a child under 14 is involved, a legal guardian may exercise these rights, though our Services are not directed at children.

## 8. Security measures

We apply administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including: access to customer systems limited to named staff with individual accounts and multi-factor authentication; logging of operator access; encryption in transit; least-privilege access to billing records; staff confidentiality commitments; and an internal personal-information management plan.
